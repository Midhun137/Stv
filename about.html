Our Story

Built on Trust, Engineered for Safety

STV Pile Foundation was established with a clear mission — to deliver superior piling solutions with uncompromising quality and safety. Based in Chennai, we have grown to serve clients across Tamil Nadu, Kerala, and Andhra Pradesh.

Our team of experienced engineers and skilled operators bring decades of combined expertise to every project. We are committed to on-time delivery and full IS code compliance.

What We Offer

Our Core Capabilities

Pile Foundation

Strong pile foundation works designed to support heavy loads with maximum safety and engineering precision.

Rotary Piling

High-precision rotary piling using modern hydraulic rigs for deep foundations in any soil condition.

Bored Piling

Low vibration bored piling ideal for urban sites and areas with restricted access.

Load Testing

Static and dynamic pile load tests ensuring design compliance and structural safety.

Soil Investigation

Complete geotechnical analysis to determine soil bearing capacity and foundation requirements.

Foundation Consulting

Expert technical guidance for foundation design, engineering, and execution oversight.

How We Work

Our Working Process

01

02
