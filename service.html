Our Services

What We Do

Complete Foundation Solutions

From soil investigation to final pile testing — we handle every stage of your foundation project with precision.

Bored Cast-in-Situ Piles

Precision bored piling solutions suitable for residential, commercial, and industrial projects using modern equipment and strict quality control processes.

Hydraulic Rotary Piling

Advanced rotary piling technology for deep foundations, ensuring speed, accuracy, and safety even in difficult and variable soil conditions.

Static & Dynamic Load Testing

Reliable pile load testing services conducted as per IS standards to verify bearing capacity, structural safety, and design compliance.

Soil Investigation

Complete geotechnical investigation including boreholes, sampling, and lab testing to design safe and economical foundation solutions.

Foundation Engineering Support

Technical guidance and execution support for foundation works, ensuring compliance with engineering standards and local building codes.

Project Execution & Supervision

End-to-end execution with experienced supervisors to ensure timely completion, safety compliance, and consistent quality workmanship.
